Archive Reference: OBS-Δ/1187
Subject: Convergent Obligation Hypothesis
Classification: Restricted Observation

Every civilization eventually invents the same warning.

Keep your word.

They disagree only on why.

Some call it karma. Others divine justice, cosmic balance, fate, or simple consequence. The names change; the certainty does not. Independent emergence across isolated civilizations suggests a common phenomenon rather than shared philosophy.

REDACTED proposes an alternative.

There exists an interval that belongs to no chronology. It cannot be measured because measurement itself does not accompany it. Whenever an obligation becomes real—a promise, an oath, a treaty, a bargain—that instant is removed from existence long enough for a single witness to acknowledge it.

No judgment is rendered.

Somewhere beyond time, a page gains another line.

Then history continues exactly as everyone remembers it.

The entity responsible has never been observed directly. No record confirms its existence, and no witness recalls an encounter. Nevertheless, the consistency of the phenomenon has earned it a provisional designation within the Archive:

The Bound.

Whether this designation refers to a being, a process, or a law remains undetermined.

The warning, however, predates every civilization we have studied.
