### SUBJECT I — ANTHROXEN

Anthroxen of the Xalthoratatex Hive has reported a recurring increase in temperature localized to his lower thorax during periods of sleep.

The increase causes no discomfort.

Quite the opposite.

Anthroxen describes the warmth as restorative and has repeatedly informed members of the Hive that his waking flexibility has improved, his lower thoracic joints feel less rigid, and his first movements following sleep are considerably more pleasant.

The Xalthoratatex Hive initially regarded the development as a boon.

The Hive continues to regard the development as a boon.

The Hive would prefer Anthroxen stop regarding the development as conversationally significant.

He has not.

The temperature increase occurs only during sleep and has no identified physiological source.

### SUBJECT II — VALERIANNA THERASSUL

Valerianna Therassul has recently experienced an almost complete cessation of the nocturnal hot flashes that had regularly interrupted her sleep.

She has attributed the improvement to her bed.

According to Valerianna, it has become "wonderfully cool" over the last several weeks, despite no alteration to the bed, room, ventilation system, or local climate controls.

She has mentioned this to her coworkers frequently.

Her coworkers were initially pleased for her.

They remain pleased for her.

They would prefer she stop telling them how much she looks forward to going to bed.

She has not.
